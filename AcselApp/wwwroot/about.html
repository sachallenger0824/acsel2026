About ACSEL

The Asian Conference on Safety & Education in Laboratory (ACSEL) is an annual conference hosted by leading universities and institutions in Asia. It provides a platform for scientists, engineers, researchers, safety professionals, and educators to exchange knowledge and practical experience in laboratory safety, environmental health, occupational safety and health, and safety education.

Ensuring personal safety while protecting the environment is a shared responsibility of all members of universities and research institutions. ACSEL promotes safer research environments through education, risk management, regulatory compliance, technology, and international collaboration.

ACSEL 2026 will be held at Chinese Culture University in Taipei, Taiwan, on 19–20 November 2026, with the theme “AI in Accident Prevention.”

ACSEL 2026 will particularly highlight the application of artificial intelligence to risk prediction, hazard identification, safety monitoring, accident prevention, emergency response, high-risk operations, and laboratory safety education. The conference also welcomes broader research and practical experience related to occupational and laboratory safety and health.
